qconform specification v0
=========================

What the checker decides, what each decision means, and what the answer is
worth. Draft v0, 2026-08-02. Read program-format-v0.txt,
descriptor-format-v0.txt and report-format-v0.txt first. This file states the
rules and the soundness claim. Those three state the data.


1. what the checker decides
---------------------------
The checker takes one capability descriptor and one pulse program. It decides
whether the device the descriptor describes can realize the program.

It answers with one of four exit codes.

  0  pass                every checked rule holds
  1  fail                at least one fatal rejection
  2  pass_with_repairs   rejections, all vendor_repairable
  3  tool error          the question could not be asked

Exit 0 and exit 2 are both realizable. Exit 2 says the toolchain will change
the program before running it, and names what it changes.

Exit 3 is not a verdict. It means the input was malformed, the descriptor was
invalid, or the checker met an arithmetic condition it refuses to guess
through. A tool error is never a statement about the device.


2. the rules
------------
Fourteen rules, in registry order. The registry is append-only: an id is never
renamed, renumbered or reused, and a retired id keeps its slot.

  R1   pulse_length_range      a play on a drive channel, in channel units,
                               outside the declared min or max
  R2   pulse_length_grid       a play duration not a whole number of channel
                               cycles
  R3   readout_length_range    a capture outside the declared min or max
  R4   frequency_range         a frame frequency outside the declared band.
                               Where the constraint is post_mixer, the band is
                               compared against the frequency minus the
                               channel mixer, because that is what the device
                               receives
  R5   frequency_resolution    a frequency that is not a whole multiple of the
                               declared step
  R6   phase_resolution        a phase that is not a whole multiple of the
                               declared step
  R7   amplitude_range         a constant amplitude outside the declared range
  R8   amplitude_resolution    an amplitude that is not a whole multiple of
                               the declared step
  R9   envelope_sample_grid    a sample count that is not a whole multiple of
                               the declared grid
  R10  envelope_amplitude      a sample whose magnitude exceeds the declared
                               maximum
  R11  envelope_memory         envelopes on one channel exceeding its memory
  R12  schedule_grid           an output starting off the timing grid, or a
                               barrier that does not align exactly on a member
                               channel lattice
  R13  negative_duration       a duration below zero
  R14  negative_time           reserved. A channel unit must be positive, a
                               negative duration does not advance the clock,
                               and a barrier takes a maximum over values that
                               are already at least zero, so the frame clock
                               cannot go negative. Nothing emits this rule

Three budget classes carry whole-program limits: pmem_words, wmem_words and
loop_registers. A budget reports fits, violates or indeterminate. A violation
is fatal. An indeterminate budget is never a rejection, because a fatal answer
must come from a proven lower bound and not from an estimate.


3. severity, and what a repair means
------------------------------------
Every rejection is fatal or vendor_repairable.

  fatal              the toolchain refuses the program, or accepts it and
                     fails later at board load
  vendor_repairable  the toolchain accepts the program and changes it

A vendor_repairable rejection names the repair. A fatal one does not. The
checker enforces that pairing when it loads a descriptor: a rule whose emit
site never reports a repair must be declared fatal, and one that always
reports a repair must be declared vendor_repairable.

A repair is not a warning about style. It says the program that runs is not
the program that was written.


4. coverage, and what pass does not mean
----------------------------------------
Every report carries a coverage manifest naming all eighteen classes as
checked, not_applicable, unchecked or indeterminate.

pass means exactly this: no violation was found among the classes marked
checked, against the echoed descriptor at its pinned versions. It means
nothing else.

  not_applicable   the program does not exercise the class
  unchecked        the descriptor declares the channel but constrains
                   nothing on it, so the checker verified nothing there
  indeterminate    a budget whose limit falls between the bounds

A channel with no constraints is declared, not checked. The manifest says so
rather than letting a silent pass imply otherwise.


5. soundness
------------
The claim is one direction only.

  If the checker returns pass or pass_with_repairs, the vendor toolchain
  compiles the program.

It is relative to a descriptor at its pinned firmware and library versions. A
descriptor with different pins is a different descriptor and carries no claim
from this one.

The converse does not hold and is not claimed. The checker may refuse a
program the toolchain compiles. Three such cases are known, and the descriptor
documents each one as a vendor_behavior.

  an out-of-band frequency that the device aliases
  a gain past full scale that the device cannot represent
  an envelope past channel memory that compiles and fails at board load

In each the toolchain accepts and the hardware does not. Refusing is the
useful answer.

Measured against asm_v2 on 2026-08-02, qick 0.2.418:

  descriptor        programs   unsound passes   coverage
  testbench              110                0      15/15
  qce2025-r26            286                0      15/15

Coverage counts a class as covered when the corpus contains a program where it
fires and a program where it is checked and does not fire. Fifteen of the
eighteen classes are reachable. negative_time and loop_registers are
unreachable by construction, stated in rule R14 and in section 2.
unconstrained_channel reports unchecked by definition and so cannot fire.

The evidence is tools/differential/results/. The method is
tools/differential/, and tests/golden/run.sh runs the frozen corpus against
any implementation of this specification.


6. what bounds the claim
------------------------
Three limits, stated because a soundness claim with unstated limits is worth
less than a narrower one that names them.

The corpus is boundary-driven from the descriptor and supplemented with seeded
randomized programs. It finds what the declared limits imply and what random
composition reaches. It does not prove absence.

Muxed generators and static readouts are outside the evidence. The program
format cannot name the tones of a muxed generator. The differential harness
refuses to lower a program that plays on one, rather than guess a tone mask.
Descriptors declare those channels, and the measurement above does not cover
them.

The claim covers one vendor. asm_v2 is the only oracle this specification has
been measured against.
